🗺️ How it works

Four simple steps to a big adventure

No boring forms, no tricky tests. Just games, a map to explore, and a report that helps grown-ups understand how a child thinks and feels.

🧒

Say hello

Enter a name, age and grade, then pick a fun avatar for the journey.

📷

Switch on the magic camera

A quick look at the star calibrates gaze tracking. Video never leaves the device.

🎮

Play 50 missions

Reading and listening adventures, picture talks you answer out loud, finger maths, lightning rounds and choose-your-path stories on a themed map.

📊

See the report

Abilities, personality strengths, emotions and attention, all in one friendly pop-up.

🌍 Six worlds

Every grade gets its own world

Each world has 50 missions in 5 zones, with difficulty tuned to the grade, so every child gets a fair and fun challenge.

👨‍👩‍👧 For parents & teachers

What MindQuest discovers

Behind every game is a carefully chosen psychometric signal. Children just play; you get the insight.

🧩 Abilities & personality

Ten thinking skills from the CHC model of intelligence, including reading and idea fluency, the Big Five personality traits and emotional intelligence, each measured by specific games instead of a questionnaire.

Safe, private and kind by design

  • 🔒 Video never leaves the device. All face and hand analysis runs inside the browser.
  • 🎤 Voice answers are optional. The browser turns speech into text (in Chrome this uses Google's speech service). Recordings stay only until the page is closed, and typing always works instead.
  • 💾 Progress is saved even if the page is refreshed, so nothing is lost.
  • 🧹 One tap clears everything. End Session wipes all data from this device.
  • 💛 No wrong-answer stress. Gentle feedback keeps every child confident.
