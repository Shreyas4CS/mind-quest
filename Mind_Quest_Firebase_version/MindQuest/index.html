How it works

Four simple steps, every week

No boring forms and no tricky tests. Just a weekly adventure that helps grown-ups understand how a child feels, thinks and copes.

Make your profile

Create a username and password, then tell Coggy your age, class, hobbies, favourite things and what feels scary.

Play the weekly quest

About 20 to 25 minutes of story videos, "What would you do?" moments, focus and memory games and calm breaths.

Talk with Coggy

Coggy reads every question aloud and listens to spoken answers. Typing always works too.

Grown-ups get guidance

Teachers and parents see well-being, focus and time, compare marks, and get practical next steps.

Weekly quests

A new adventure every week

Each quest is built on the areas of the CBSE-style Holistic Well-being Report Card, with the wording tuned to the child's stage.

Six worlds

Every grade gets its own world

The child's dashboard takes on the colours and doodles of their grade's world.

For teachers & parents

What MindQuest shows you

Children just play. You get a clear picture and kind, practical next steps: what to say, what to try at home, books to read and places to visit.

12 well-being areas

Each area is scored on the familiar 4-step scale: Developing, Progressing, Consistent, Strength.

One clear dashboard

Every number has an "i" that explains it. Flags show who needs a check-in first.

Safe, private and kind by design

  • Video never leaves the device. Facial expressions are read inside the browser; only counts are saved.
  • Private accounts. Each child sees only their own quests. Results are for their teacher and parents.
  • Voice is optional. Every answer can be typed instead.
  • A screening picture, not a diagnosis. Gentle flags help adults start a caring conversation.
